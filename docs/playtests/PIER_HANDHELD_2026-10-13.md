# The borrowed rod: pending human playtest

Use the current integrated game through PLAY_FIRSTLIGHT_WINDOWS.cmd and the same browser profile/origin you normally play. Automated qualification uses separate synthetic profiles; it never inspects personal saves.

At the lantern pier, open the choices with E and choose Cast a line. Look along the line is an explicit camera action. Hook when the bite appears, reel when the fish turns, and ease during its stronger pull. After a catch, choose Release silverfin. Try V during reeling to compare third person and Diorama, then try reduced motion in Settings. Try this once on a fresh character and once with an existing blade or bow.

There is no bait cost, XP, money or kept fish. Equipped weapons stay owned and stowed; this outing does not advance story. The cast is cosmetic inside the same bite wait. Walking away or reloading puts the borrowed rod away.
