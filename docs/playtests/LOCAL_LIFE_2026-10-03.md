# Local life across the roads

This review slice adds practical, finite work to the existing four non-Earth openings. Earth keeps Stormfall and the Living Road, its patrols and Trailward binding. These are local adaptations of recovered realm design; their provisional quest titles do not establish the endings of the larger canon.

Start the game through the existing loopback launcher, using the same browser origin for your existing character. Backups and personal saves remain yours. New test footage uses isolated profiles and imported command-earned characters.

All four commissions require Oren's initial expedition kit. Heaven also requires the claimed Broken Choir repair; Hell also requires the claimed Neris rescue. Atlantis and Cosmos have no additional campaign prerequisite.

Open **J → Journal → Local life across the roads** to read each commission. Read the arrangement, route and payment before choosing. Use **Roads** to visit the relevant region; read the separate local commission at its giver. Blue L labels on **M** show available physical work. Walking moves you to work; it never records an objective. **E**, or the work panel beside the site, performs an available action. Supplied parts travel separately from ordinary inventory and equipment.

For Atlantis, enter at Tide Steps. **F** ascends and **G** descends; releasing holds depth. The upper gauge requires actual water at foot height −1.05 m. Continue through the maintained doorway into Bellglass's air court, at foot height −2.70 m. Leave the court through the same doorway, then take the east water lane to the far dry gallery landing. Press **E** there to exit before walking back to Sahra. Returning directly to Firstlight also remains free. The supplied visitor protection has no breath timer; this lamp does not create the court's air.

Choose deliberately at acceptance. An unsupported assembly spends nothing and explains why it cannot fit. Work, chosen arrangement, supplied cargo and unpaid completion persist. A successful claim pays once; reopening, reload and a changed request ID cannot pay it again. If your wallet/material capacity or storage refuses the whole payment, the completed job stays unpaid for retry. Reopening starts at your saved home checkpoint; revisit the same realm to resume.

Nothing auto-equips, consumes your timber/ore/crops as quest parts, erases a socket, heals you or grants a new weapon tier. The combined payment is 92 XP, 33 sunmarks, 2 ore, 4 timber, 5 fibre and 1 crystal, subject to available room under the existing stored XP cap. These are contributions to the established crafting/building catalogue. A character with fully fitted strongest gear receives useful ordinary materials and currency, rather than an invented scaling power reward.

Press **V** to swap third person and diorama at any point. Installed work remains in the world in either view. Pending human questions: **Did you know where to go? Did the places feel more inhabited? Could you recognize your chosen finished work? Did you want to return?** Also check whether the swimming route and refusal messages were clear. Scripted known-route footage and automated checks do not answer those questions.

Existing close orbits are preserved. If the refuge wall fills your view, press **R** to reset the current framing or orbit normally. With cutaway enabled, the Cosmos roof reveals the work when you stand under its footprint and the camera is above it; turning cutaway off retains the complete shelter. Automated appearance comparisons use deliberately reset views and do not promise every viewing angle is unobstructed.
