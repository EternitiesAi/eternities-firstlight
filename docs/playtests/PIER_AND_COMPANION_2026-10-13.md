# A quiet outing at home

This is a new optional activity in the existing Firstlight Valley. It does not
require campaign progress or change the rewards of Oren's outing. Names and
fishing tuning remain provisional design choices informed by the founder's
Earth charter. Human enjoyment has not been established by automated runs.

Launch `PLAY_FIRSTLIGHT_WINDOWS.cmd`, or run `python tools/play_local.py` in
the reviewed checkout. Keep the usual browser/profile and local origin for
your own saves. Use an empty character slot for a fresh case; no personal
world needs to be replaced or imported for this playtest.

1. Walk to the end of the Lantern pier, beyond the Hearthwater trail marker.
2. Press E and choose **Cast a line**. **Rest beside the water** is still a
   separate choice. Opening the choices does not record either action.
3. **Look along the line** frames the water in your current camera style.
   V still swaps third person and Diorama; framing is an explicit choice,
   keeps the FOV and remembers the chosen view like other camera actions.
4. Watch for a bite. It waits for you: use **Hook** or E when ready.
5. Reel as the fish turns, and ease during a strong pull. E switches reel/ease;
   the primary button also works by keyboard. Holding the Reel button reels
   until released. Too much tension or prolonged slack lets the fish escape.
6. A caught lantern silverfin stays beside the pier until you explicitly
   release it. There is no money, XP, bait cost, inventory item or saved catch.
   You may cast another line deliberately. Walking away or leaving the page
   puts the borrowed rod away. P, blocking menus and photo mode suspend fishing
   time. H enters photo mode; H or Escape returns without releasing the fish.
   E does not operate a line hidden behind photo or menu controls. The nearby
   shore action in Explore opens the same explicit choices.

Try once with a fresh character and once with existing stronger equipment.
Equipment and campaign history should remain yours. Fishing ends on reload;
it is not an unfinished quest or a hidden reward waiting to be claimed.

If Briar has been rescued, stop on the nearby path and let Follow settle. The
fox should make space beside you. For the existing cloth-and-doorstep outing,
stand at the ordinary house approach while Briar rests beside the door. A
crowded or displaced fox cannot be remembered as an arrived resting moment.

The useful human questions are simple: Did you find the activity and understand
the line? Could you see the float and read the fish in either camera? Did this
feel like a pleasant place to spend time with your companion?

This is a small original rod-in-holder presentation. Hand casting/reeling
animation, fishing audio, other species, collecting and an economic profession
are not implemented by this change. A successful automated catch is not a
measured human completion time, a performance result or proof of enjoyment.
