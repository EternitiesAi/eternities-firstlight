# Lower Garden campaign play guide

The current arc is **The Gate That Remained Open**, available after obtaining the expedition kit and explicitly claiming **The Broken Choir**. It is a lower Garden prototype continuation, separate from Heaven01 and the sanctuary. Existing characters stay themselves; no class, soul choice or campaign chapter is forced.

Open the journal and read the Heaven campaign invitation. Travel to the lower Garden, walk to Rielle and accept its exact danger and once-only fee. Follow the account, maker's marks and false signal. The grounding mirror is optional supplied work. Begin the approach watch deliberately. Tab/click selects each real apparatus; stationary attacks, bow obstruction, Brace and retreat retain their usual controls. The first construct warns with a directed beam; the relay warns with a circular pulse. Both can be damaged throughout combat. Disable them, then fit the service signs.

At the waiting bell courier, deliberately invite the walk. Close the journal to let the actual world resume and accompany the courier along the marked western loop. It waits when you get too far ahead. Reaching Calen alone does not complete this objective. Leaving or reloading resets an unfinished walk, so return to the waiting place and explicitly invite again. If an arrival save is refused, stay beside the actual courier and Calen with the workspace closed while the save callback retries; it remains unpaid until saved.

Fit the returned arrival instrument, inspect both arrangements and deliberately confirm one. Both keep the public road open and preserve the original repair. Try the instrument: its short stroke returns to rest and grants nothing. Verify the actual welcome at Calen, return to Rielle and explicitly claim the entire fee. A full pouch keeps the verified work unpaid and retryable. Neither arrangement auto-equips anything.

V switches between third-person and diorama. Try each apparatus warning, courier bend and retained instrument in both views. Automated known-route journeys do not supply founder taste, first-time pacing or human enjoyment.

Pending human questions: Did the account and map make the next action clear? Did the beam and pulse feel different? Was the actual courier pleasant to accompany rather than frustrating? Did the final instrument and recognition feel like a lasting welcome? Does either camera obscure the route or contact warning? No human answers have been recorded for this arc.
