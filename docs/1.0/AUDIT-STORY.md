# story audit, 2026-11-13 (opus 5.5 aide, condensed by the lead)

## the story as played before 1.0

1. oren's riverbank outing (three supplies, old bristle).
2. the mine: drive off a skitter, rescue the injured fox briar (`src/adventure.js:262`).
3. the hollow hart (240 hp, `adventure.js:41`), the feather on its dais (`:268`).
4. the envoy at the spring: the best writing in the game (`adventure.js:389-391`): "I was told I would find a fortress…
   Instead, I found someone who knows how to begin."
5. sunward road: tessa's caravan, the cart, the beacon (`:272-304`).
6. bellweather: rowan's bell, briar sniffs out the clapper, the hushbound keeper (410 hp, `crossing.js:44`).
7. earth roads: fenna's delivery, the living-road allocation, coastward work.
8. hell: the tithe-warden veyr, then unbind / divert / license (`hell-campaign-data.js:65`).
9. heaven: glasswing construct and relay, escort a courier, choose a bell-plate arrangement.
10. atlantis: set inlet, equalizer and outlet valves for damar.
11. cosmos: avar's synchronization, the still meridian guardian (`cosmos-campaign-data.js:144`).
12. homecoming: gated on twelve earlier claims; isolate two relays, "repel" the regent of ash (168 hp); "keep exploring."

what was wrong: the prose was compliance text ("this records a victory, not a claim that he was killed"); every realm read
like a maintenance ticket (compare records, fit a supplied part, choose a disposition, claim the fee); the difficulty curve
ran backwards (the final boss weaker than chapter iv's); briar stopped mattering after bellweather; nothing earlier came
back (the envoy, the feather, the prophecy); realms were separate errands; nobody had played it.

## the canonical arc (archive branch `origin/archive/story-bible-and-living-canticle-2026-09-13`: draft-b bible §4-5,
draft-c living canticle §3-4, VISION_QUESTIONS.md)

the regent of ash builds a counterfeit road network that turns every passage into ownership. the prophecy: "the first
light will not descend from heaven. it will rise from below." briar dies saving travellers and is won back in the
nearshore by refusing a perfect fake briar (1.0 default: briar is taken, not killed). heaven is seen before it is
defended. hell has a foundry where the stolen pattern is recovered. the cosmos shows no single master key should hold the
roads. the finale is a three-phase regent fight at a home junction with allies the player earned, ending in a "first
answer". missing before 1.0: briar's loss and return, the regent as a person met through the game, the hell foundry,
heaven's peaceful interior and the envoy's home, michael/gabriel/uriel, the prophecy spoken and reread, the feather as a
tool, regional allies at the finale, the epilogue (ilan's concert, oren's workshop), the answering as more than a line.

## signature moments proposed

the envoy's first lines, then the valley through the feather (counterfeit roads glowing ash red you never noticed);
briar's loss and the quiet after; heaven's first minutes with no combat, then rescue corridors turning hostile; the false
briar in the nearshore; the finale in the merged valley, the camera rising from third person to the diorama as the last
hit lands; the echo ledger (the regent quotes your own choices back as chains; the people you freed break them).
