# Actual canvas and app-audio recording

`tools/canvas_film.py` records the existing game canvas and the actually opted-in Web Audio master in one browser MediaRecorder stream. It observes the original master-to-destination connection before startup, adds one removable recording-only destination and removes its own tap/tracks at completion. No sound is replaced, synthesized offline for the video, retimed or passed off as a successful combat event.

`tools/capture_realm_trails.py --sound-video` keeps the separate full-UI Playwright video and adds `canvas-with-app-audio.webm`. The full-UI video includes menus/HUD and is silent. The additional stream contains the actual canvas and synchronized app audio; it excludes DOM overlays. Both follow the same ordinary input/RAF session. This optional recording tool does not enable the user's sound setting, touch personal profiles or alter game/save rules outside the isolated capture session.

The initial ten-second isolated home smoke used Chromium 143.0.7499.4 with ANGLE NVIDIA RTX 3080 D3D11 at 1280×720. Actual sound opt-in preceded capture. HTML SHA256 was `8956b50ad67549e246f094a520c5bb9f7c7aa817308a1970e27272934f5bf2f7`; this is an earlier development epoch, not final integrated footage. There were no artificial simulation ticks, position edits, inventory grants or external soundtrack. The raw WebM is 5,913,460 bytes, SHA256 `5860bae04688a9805578c85fddcc82f2e89f67814090a2c9822ea4dae21eb471`, for 10.0177 seconds of browser recording.

FFmpeg 7.1 decoded all **289 video frames** and **478,080 mono analysis samples at 48kHz**, with audio RMS 0.01355 and peak 0.04863. Initial default/null-output decodes warned about five non-monotonic DTS values caused by coarse output frame-timebase quantization; those logs remain. Explicit millisecond output timebase (`-fps_mode passthrough -enc_time_base:v 1:1000 -f null -`) decoded the complete stream with zero warnings/errors. Exit codes, streams and decoded counts are retained, rather than hiding the initial warnings. Base64 extraction splits the data URL at `;base64,`; splitting at the first comma would cut the codec list in the MIME type and corrupt the tool's output.

Heavy raw capture and decode receipts remain in `D:/07-GAMES/Firstlight/artifacts/coastward-banks-2026-10-02/canvas-audio-smoke-01/`. This proves the recording path and a bounded nonzero real audio signal. It does not establish listening quality, speakers, sustained game/GPU performance, final gameplay acceptance or a public deployment. Current-epoch normal-time outings will retain their own hashes and receipts.
