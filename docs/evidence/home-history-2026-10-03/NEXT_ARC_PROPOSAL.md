# A useful return across the bridge

This is a bounded source-grounded proposal from the read-only Earth colleague,
recorded by Root after the home milestone. It is not implemented by PR40 and does
not establish new founder canon. Root recommends using it as the next slice,
subject to fresh ownership/geometry inspection when implementation begins.

Merren's existing settlement and Vessa's existing bridge bank can share a finite
service of returnable field frames. The player deliberately accepts one supplied
batch, fits its dividers and marked load at the supported store frontage, carries
it along the existing road/long bridge, delivers its contents to Vessa, and brings
the empty frames home. Choosing an open rack or supplied covered nest leaves a
modest visible arrangement. Merren recognizes the specific completed batch.

This should not spend ordinary equipment/inventory as job parts, introduce a
spoilage timer, force a class or provide automatic story consent. Carried pieces
must be visible on the actual actor and transfer through accepted nearby events.
Rejected packing or saving must preserve partial progress and all old ledgers.

The proposed bounded economy is three separately accepted rounds per character,
each displaying4 sunmarks and2 fibre before acceptance, with no XP or ore.
Fibre is useful to existing finite fittings/home recipes without granting new
equipment ownership. After three explicit claims, the rack/recognition remain
and paid offers stop. This is a candidate tuning choice, not approved pricing or
the permanent rule for future settlement services. Each round needs a stable
definition/run/claim identity independent of request IDs and earlier payouts.

The proposed authority is a small `earth-settlement-rounds` module and optional
`settlementRounds1` ledger, with narrowly scoped UI/art and the existing durable
save adapter. Missing-field migration should begin unaccepted, preserve stored
XP and all prior claims, and never backfill a payment. Candidate coordinates are
Merren(-6,-68), Vessa(-7,97), and a store-front work anchor(-7,-73.5); the prop,
body clearance and route must be measured in production before adopting them.
Closed house doors remain closed. Names are existing provisional implementation
names, not new canon-approved branding.

Acceptance needs actual production movement in both cameras with body support,
an independently available home return, nearby validated packing/handoffs, visible
carried/rack geometry, and explicit once-per-round payment. Partial work,
completed-unpaid work and selected rack arrangement must reload with their exact
round identity. Capacity/save refusal must preserve the complete wallet,
inventory, ownership and old ledgers; a successful retry pays the whole declared
bundle once. Appearance controls must restore state/batches/framebuffer and keep
instance counts bounded. A real normal-time route recording is distinct from
accelerated engineering checks and deferred human enjoyment.

The open founder question is whether later settlement services become authored
finite batches or open-ended repeat work. The capped experiment can leave that
unresolved. Paid power, offline loss, rare pets, construction scale, the forest
organism's larger story and the final campaign remain separate decisions.
