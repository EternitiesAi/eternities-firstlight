# Five journeys from one home

Launch `PLAY_FIRSTLIGHT_WINDOWS.cmd` from this gameplay checkout. It serves the game at `http://127.0.0.1:8780/`; use the same browser and profile as your previous local game to resume its characters. Changing the port or profile gives a different save origin. Export a character from **Character → Characters** when you want a portable backup. You can create another character there if one of the three slots is free, without replacing your returning one.

At Oren's outdoor workbench, collect the initial expedition kit. **K → Walk to Oren; once you arrive, press K again → Collect supplies** is one visible route. No campaign chapter, soul power, class choice or old realm survey is required for the new work. A blade comes with the kit. Bow users can craft the existing trail bow at the workbench and deliberately equip it in Character.

Walk to **Roads of Light** near the workshop. **J → Roads → Read this opening** describes each destination. At the marker, choose **Read crossing terms**, then **Save checkpoint & cross**. Once there, meet the local giver and accept the deeper optional trail. Its route, danger and fixed reward are visible before you accept. The ordinary visitor surveys are separate repeatable jobs.

**M** shows available actions along the local route; **J → Roads** shows the accepted trail and next action. Dry objectives can offer a walk to a nearby supported spot. Interact with **E**. Supplies belong to the job rather than taking equipment slots or consuming your existing timber, crops or stone.

**Tab** or click selects a foe. **1** toggles stationary autoattack; it does not chase the foe through walls. **3** braces. Heaven's core opens after its pulse: a closed bearing blocks arrows, and the optional spillway gives a longer recovery. Hell's rescue begins only after the route is stabilized. Stay within ten paces of Neris, return to her if she waits, and use the explicit Wait/Resume controls. Your arrival alone cannot complete hers. Assisted extraction is offered separately and recorded as assistance.

In Atlantis, first walk to **Tide Steps · optional gallery entry**, press **E**, and choose **Enter the visitor gallery**. Once inside, **WASD** swims, **F** rises and **G** descends. Releasing them holds your measured depth. Read the foot-depth display and enter the air court through its actual doorway. There is no air countdown in this prototype. In Cosmos, turn the slider or use its arrow keys until the split scale aligns; then explicitly record. An unrecorded preview is lost on reload.

Return to the giver and deliberately claim the completed job. A full reward pouch or refused save leaves it unpaid and retryable. Reload returns you to your home checkpoint; your accepted work remains and can be resumed by revisiting the destination. Each new trail pays once. The older routine surveys retain their declared repeat rewards.

After any claimed trail, the outdoor home workbench offers **one realm-work fitting per character**. It costs 3 ore and 8 sunmarks and adds 3 attack to one owned blade or bow. Inspect the before/after comparison before spending. It keeps the weapon, socket and previous upgrades, and never equips an item for you. Other trails do not grant more fittings. Try the result against Oren's practice bundle.

**V** swaps third person and your last diorama view. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and **R** resets the current view. Both styles keep their own framing. Return to Firstlight remains available; larger continents, faction endings and seamless open-world streaming are still unfinished.

At Coastward's **The channel crossing** map point, press **E → Frame the channel bridge** for the supported side view. Then keep walking. It retains the selected style and field of view; **V** offers the other framing and **R** resets the current one. Third person includes the distant mountain skyline; the tighter diorama frames the deck and water. A shallow view at the near rail edge can still obscure part of the legs.

The top music-note control opts into app sound. The new quiet ambience follows the current realm and Atlantis's body medium, and pauses with reading menus. Use Settings for reduced motion or camera cutaway preferences. Briar's nearby decorative tag stays out of people's interaction labels; the actual Follow/Stay controls remain beside you.

If the launcher reports that port 8780 serves a different build, close that old Firstlight preview and launch this checkout again. It refuses to replace another server or silently move your save origin. An earlier automatic review rejected Codex's attempt to restart the old preview before execution; the existing preview and personal profile remain untouched.

For a fresh and a returning playthrough, the useful questions are: Did you know where to go? Did the fights feel better? Did the reward make you want another outing? The automated journeys and recordings do not answer those human questions.
